Here is the categorization of the key terms extracted from the PizzaPazza home delivery scenario, organized into Actors, Processes, and Objects.

1. Actors (Who makes an action)
Customer / Mary: The person who connects to the website, places the order, provides payment, and receives the pizzas.

Web-site: The digital platform that receives the customer's order and payment submission.

Cook / John: The kitchen staff member responsible for managing the worklist, preparing, and cooking the pizzas.

Delivery-boy / Eddy: The person responsible for knowing the streets, collecting the cooked pizzas, and delivering them to the customer.

2. Processes (The actions taken)
To fill in / to place: The action of entering and submitting the order and payment details on the website.

To produce / to make: The in-house creation of the dough and the overall craft of making pizzas.

To bake / to cook: The careful baking and cooking of the prepared pizzas in the kitchen.

To fulfill: The overall action of executing and completing the customer's order quickly.

To put (on the worklist): The action of queuing the order for preparation.

To alert: The action of signaling the delivery-boy to come pick up the pizzas.

To collect / to pick up: The action of gathering the pizzas from the kitchen to start the delivery trip.

To deliver / to reach: The action of traveling through the streets to bring the order to the customer's address.

3. Objects (The result or direct object of the action)
Order: The customer's requested items and details submitted through the website (placed on the worklist).

Payment: The financial transaction provided by the customer alongside their order.

Dough: The high-quality base material produced in-house for making the pizzas.

Pizza (Napoli pizzas): The culinary product prepared by the cook, collected by the delivery-boy, and delivered to the customer.

Worklist: The queue where incoming orders are placed before preparation.

Delivery trip: The journey undertaken by the delivery-boy to reach the customer's home.

Customer's address / Home: The final destination where the order is successfully delivered.
