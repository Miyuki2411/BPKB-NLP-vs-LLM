Here is the classification of the terms from your scenario into the three requested categories (Actor, Process, and Object):

1. Actor (Who makes an action)
Customer / Mary: The person who connects to the website, places the order, provides payment, and receives the delivery.

Shop / PizzaPazza: The business entity that receives orders and fulfills them.

Cook / John: The staff member responsible for reviewing the worklist, preparing, and cooking the pizzas.

Delivery-boy / Eddy: The staff member who knows the streets, picks up the cooked pizzas, and delivers them to the customer.

Fills in / Places / Submits: The customer's action of inputting and sending their order and payment through the website.

Produces (dough): The in-house creation of the dough required for making pizzas.

Baking / Cooks / Prepares: The culinary steps taken by the cook to make the pizzas.

Fulfill: The overall process of completing and delivering the customer's order quickly.

Alerts: The cook signaling the delivery-boy to pick up the ready pizzas.

Collects / Picks up: The delivery-boy gathering the pizzas from the kitchen.

Delivery trip / Reaches: The travel process of navigating the streets to bring the pizzas to the customer's home.

3. Object (The result of the action / Direct object)
Web-site: The digital platform used to submit orders and payments.

Order / Mary's order: The submitted request containing the details of what needs to be made.

Payment: The monetary transaction provided alongside the order.

Dough: The good-quality, in-house-made base ingredient for the pizzas.

Worklist: The queue where incoming orders are placed for the cook.

Napoli pizzas / Pizzas: The food items prepared, cooked, collected, and delivered.
