Business Process Glossary: PizzaPazza
Customer

Definition: A person (e.g., Mary) who places an order for pizzas and provides payment through the company's website.

Order

Definition: A request submitted by a customer through the website specifying the desired items (e.g., two Napoli pizzas) along with the associated payment.

Web-site

Definition: The online digital platform used by customers to submit their pizza orders and make payments to the shop.

Dough

Definition: An in-house produced mixture of flour and other ingredients that serves as the base for making pizzas.

Worklist

Definition: A queue or schedule managed by the cook where incoming customer orders are placed and ordered for preparation.

Cook

Definition: The kitchen staff member (e.g., John) responsible for managing the worklist, preparing the pizzas, and baking them.

Delivery-boy

Definition: The logistics staff member (e.g., Eddy) who knows local streets, collects ready pizzas from the shop, and delivers them directly to the customer's address.

Delivery Trip

Definition: The transit process undertaken by the delivery-boy to travel from the pizza shop to the customer's home.
